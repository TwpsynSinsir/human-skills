### What do empathy, trust and listening mean to me?

Empathy, to me, means taking the time to understand and feel what those around me are feeling. Respectfully acknowledging and reacting to the emotions and feelings of the people I'm interacting with.

Trust, to me, is straight forward: If I feel that you're an honourable person with good morals, whether I agree with your opinions or views on certain things or not, I trust you. My belief of trust is based on consistency: If someone has continuously shown me to act in an upstanding, honourable manner, I will trust them and offer my own trust in return.

Listening, and active listening are simple gestures in theory, but since coming to DAA, I've started to view them as more complicated actions that I try to give more weight to. To listen is to hear someone's thoughts, and understand what they wish to say. By this, I mean parsing what the intention behind their words are, not necessarily taking them at face value. In the context of DAA, this means treating what they say with kindness and understanding.
